About

Created by Nic Lydon

Operator Verified — named for its one claim, that an operator was verified as a unique human — is an independent open-source program building, in public, small pieces of trust infrastructure for the agent era: ways to check that the operator behind an agent was verified as a unique human. The projects are open source under Apache-2.0. This site is the program's home and its weekly public log.

No token

This program has no token. Nothing here is for sale as a token, no token is planned, and anything offered under these names as a token is not ours. The projects are open source under Apache-2.0; the site asks for no wallet, no purchase, and no sign-up to read or verify anything.

Why “Operator Verified”

The label names the event: an operator was checked, as a unique human. It does not claim anyone's identity was verified — the proof establishes that a unique human backs the operator, never who that human is. Wherever the umbrella speaks, the claim is phrased “verified as a unique human”.

Privacy commitments

Our pages and attestations do not print the raw human identifier. AgentBook registrations are public on World Chain: a disclosed wallet or published hash can reveal that identifier and other wallets registered to it. The fleet page shows labels only and does not publish its wallet roster.

Demonstrations use synthetic or dedicated wallets. This site ships with no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies; the light/dark preference is stored on your own device.

Contact

Through the public repositories' issue trackers, starting with Fiador's — Fiador is the program's human-backed attestation for Discord bots project.
