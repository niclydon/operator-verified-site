# Launch — 2026-10-26

The founding narrative closed at a preview deployment with two
gates deliberately left open: the author's approval of the exact
launch text and of devlog post 1, and a real-browser visual pass
in both color modes. Both closed on October 26, 2026.

The author reviewed the deployed preview, asked what remained
against going live, and approved the launch the same night. The
visual pass ran against the deployed preview first: home and the
Fiador page in light and dark modes, one hero plate rendering in
each, no duplication or layout breakage. Only after that did the
banner come off.

The launch commit makes five changes and no others. The preview
banner is gone from every page; the builder now emits a banner
only when one is configured, so local builds still carry their
local-preview warning by default and production builds carry
none. Devlog post 1 is published: draft flag cleared, devlog
index listing updated, and the post moved from its draft path to
its publication-date path. The first snapshot,
`snapshots/2026-10-26-launch/`, freezes the launched tree with a
hash manifest, and the snapshots index records it with its
reason. The facts file moves its generation date to launch day
and updates exactly one fact, the canonical domain, to its
launched state; the program-wallet fact still reads "prepared,
not yet executed," because it still is. This narrative and the
CHANGES.md launch entry complete the record.

The domain work closed the pass: operatorverified.org attached
to the Vercel project as canonical, with operatorverified.com,
operator-verified.com, and operator-verified.org redirecting to
it, and the production site verified live on the canonical
domain (all routes answering, banner absent, post 1 published).
The site was also added to the public showcase watchdog, so its
state is checked alongside the author's other showcase pages.

What did not happen at launch, stated as plainly as the founding
narrative stated its own limits: no announcement post was made
anywhere, and none is implied by the launch; the production OG
cards the design requires before announcement are still owed,
as is the dev.to syndication sequencing decision. The formal
USPTO search on the Operator Verified name remains outstanding;
the name is in use on the strength of the availability sweeps
recorded in the design document, not on a clearance opinion.
The program wallet's registration is still prepared and not yet
executed, awaiting the author's World App action, and the site
continues to say so.
