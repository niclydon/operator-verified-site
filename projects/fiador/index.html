Fiador

Fiador is this program's human-backed attestation for Discord bots. Fiador: in Spanish and Portuguese law, the guarantor — the person who stands surety for another. That is the operator's job for their bot: not the actor, the one who answers for the attestation.

Naming note: this repository was founded as “human-backed” on October 25, 2026 and renamed to Fiador the same day; its founding documents are being updated in a rename pass. Links: repository · spec · founding narrative

01 / WHAT IT PROVES

Proves

A unique human registered the wallet that signed the attestation binding that wallet to a Discord application.

Does not prove

That the human wrote any particular message, endorses any particular output, or controls the bot at this moment.

02 / HOW IT WORKS
  1. The operator registers their agent wallet in AgentBook with their verified World ID, and signs an attestation binding the wallet to their Discord application.
  2. The attestation is published where a checker can fetch it. It carries a hash of the operator's human identifier — never the identifier itself.
  3. A checker (a self-hosted verifier bot, or the no-install verify page) will verify the signature, check expiry, and resolve the wallet live in AgentBook.
03 / STATUS AND EVIDENCE

What exists today: the protocol spec, this design record, and the badge artwork below. What does not exist yet, named plainly: the packaged verifier bot running in a public guild, and a registry listing. The path it will run has already been proven end to end: on October 26, 2026, a World App proof verified through the verification bot and assigned the Verified Human role in a live Discord server, and this site carries a static page explaining the check. The verify package is designed to live in this repo.

Evidence

Substrate prototype, October 25, 2026. The verification loop was proven end to end against a real AgentBook registration in a private prototype: three granted uses, and the fourth denied when the trial allowance exhausted, as designed. That run used a playground wallet, which is not used in public material.

Program wallet. A dedicated wallet was generated for this program only: 0x21BD…A14Bc (truncated; the truncated form is the only public form). Its registration is registered (confirmed live); the founding attestation and any registry listing will use this wallet.
